Ravi had always been afraid of the old banyan tree at the edge of the village, the one the elders said was haunted.
One evening, chasing his runaway kite, he found himself standing right beneath its twisted roots as the sun went down.
Instead of ghosts, he found a family of owls nesting in its hollow, their eyes glowing like little lanterns in the dark.
The oldest owl tilted its head at him, almost like it was smiling, and Ravi realized he was more curious than scared.
He sat quietly until his kite drifted down on its own, caught gently in the low branches above him.
Walking home under the stars, Ravi decided the tree wasn't haunted at all, it was just misunderstood, like a lot of things people feared without ever looking closely.
