The Man Who Gave Water to a Thirsty Dog

One day, a man was walking through the desert.

The sun was burning hot, and the ground beneath his feet was dry.

After walking for a long time, he became extremely thirsty.

Finally, he found a well.

He climbed down into the well, drank some water, and climbed back out.

But as he looked around, he noticed a dog.

The dog was exhausted from thirst.

It was walking around the well, licking the wet ground, desperately searching for water.

The man looked at the dog and thought:

"This animal is suffering from thirst just as I was."

He went back down into the well.

He filled his shoe with water.

Then he climbed back up and gave the water to the dog.

The dog drank until its thirst was gone.

The man continued on his journey.

But he didn't know that this small act of kindness had changed everything.

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ told his companions that Allah forgave this man because of what he did for the thirsty dog.

The companions asked:

"Messenger of Allah, do we receive a reward for showing kindness to animals?"

He ﷺ replied that there is a reward for kindness to every living creature.

And that is the lesson:

Never think that a small act of kindness is worthless.

A little water.

A little food.

A helping hand.

A kind word.

You may forget the good deed...

But Allah never forgets.

Sometimes, the smallest act done sincerely for Allah can become the reason for His mercy.

So today, look for one small opportunity to do good.

You never know how much it may mean to Allah.
